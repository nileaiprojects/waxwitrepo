About Waxwit

Marketing that feels human.

Waxwit was built on one belief — that great marketing should feel human.

Founded by Muskan Kapoor after 9 years of building brands across India, the UK, the US and Australia, Waxwit works with businesses that have something real to say and need the right person to help them say it. We've grown Instagram accounts to 850,000 followers, led marketing across 60+ retail stores and delivered 150+ projects across fashion, real estate, fintech, hospitality and lifestyle.

We don't post for the sake of posting. Every piece of content we create has a purpose.
